Built-In Safeguards

How the Planner Keeps Every Layout Safe

SproutSpace turns ECDA and SCDF licensing requirements into live, visual rules. Each placement is checked against the selected age group, so a layout cannot be submitted for approval until it is age-appropriate, visible, and evacuable.

Age-appropriate spacing

Each age group carries its own ratio and floor-area rules. Selecting Kindergarten K1 sets the minimum per-child area and caps furniture density, so the room never exceeds the licensed capacity for that band - from Infant Care through K2.

Supervision & visibility

The planner traces the educator’s line of sight and flags any furniture over 1.2 m that would create a blind spot. Tall storage is pushed to the perimeter so children remain visible from the circle area at all times.

Clear evacuation paths

SCDF rules require unobstructed routes to every exit. The planner keeps a minimum walkway width clear of furniture and confirms at least two independent paths reach the door before a layout can be submitted.

Mapped to safety KPIs

Every check feeds the sandbox KPI of 100% first-time classroom-layout safety approval, while reusable, pre-validated templates cut planning time by 50% - Centre Directors approve digitally rather than re-measuring on paper.

Why it matters

By the time a layout reaches a Centre Director, the hard safety questions are already answered. Educators spend their energy on the pedagogy of the space - provocations, flow between zones, quiet versus active areas - not on chasing compliance, and HQ gains a consistent, evidence-based record of every room across the network.
